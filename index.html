ACCOUNT & DATA CONTROLS

Delete your account
and associated data.

You can request permanent deletion of your Scene Ready account here. You do not need to install, reinstall, or open the app.

Request account deletion

Email support with the email address registered to your Scene Ready account. If possible, send the request from that address.

Email a deletion request

No email app opens? Send your request directly to
kerrsammi1993@gmail.com

We verify account ownership before processing email requests and confirm when deletion is complete. Sending an email does not immediately delete your account.

If you cannot access your registered email, explain that in your request. Additional ownership verification may be needed. Never send passwords, verification codes, health information, or progress photos by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your Scene Ready account, login identity, and profile.
  • Your training intake responses, waiver acknowledgments, and bookings.
  • Your habits, check-ins, streaks, rewards, and associated accountability records.
  • Your uploaded private progress photos, including photos you chose to save.

What to expect

Email requests are reviewed manually. Deletion begins after ownership is verified, and we confirm completion by email.

Successful deletion removes your account data from live Scene Ready services. The 90-day cleanup period for unsaved photos does not apply to account deletion: saved and unsaved account photos are removed.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Already have the app?

You can also start deletion directly in Scene Ready:

  1. Sign in and open Profile.
  2. Select Delete my account and data.
  3. Read the warning and confirm permanent deletion.

The app starts processing immediately after confirmation. If it reports an error, do not assume deletion finished; contact the email above. The email-request option remains available without the app.

Scope and retention

Scene Ready does not intentionally retain your live account records or uploaded private photos after successful deletion. Records belonging to other users may remain, with references to your account removed where applicable.

Deletion cannot remove copies previously downloaded to a device. Infrastructure backups and operational logs, where present, follow the hosting provider’s retention policies and are not erased by the live-account deletion operation.
